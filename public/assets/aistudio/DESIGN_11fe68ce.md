## Brand & Style

This design system expresses an ethereal, high-end editorial aesthetic tailored for sophisticated digital portfolios and studios. It deliberately contrasts deep, cosmic obsidian black with luminous blush pinks and tactile, warm porcelain tones. The visual identity bridges technical rigor with haute-couture editorial grace, evoking wonder, precision, and artistic elevation.

### Design Principles & Visual Language
- **Luminous Atmospheric Depth:** Rather than relying on traditional drop shadows, depth is manifested through volumetric backlights, radial rim-lighting, and ethereal rose-pink glows reminiscent of spun silk and butterfly wings.
- **Sectional Sandwiching:** The spatial rhythm alternates deliberately between immersive obsidian dark canvases (in the opening hero and grounding footer) and high-clarity, warm blush-pink bento surfaces across core content areas.
- **Editorial Poise:** High-contrast serif headlines lend literary stature and timeless authority, balanced by razor-sharp humanist sans-serif UI typography engineered for clarity.
- **Sculpted Tactility:** Rounded containers, pill-shaped interactive triggers, and frosted glass elements feel physical, soft to the touch, and meticulously framed.

## Colors

The palette is anchored in an interplay between deep obsidian shadows and warm pastel blushes. The design system functions primarily as an editorial light experience set on a soft blush canvas (`#F6ECEB`), bookended by deep espresso-obsidian (`#151011`) stages.

### Color Hierarchy & Roles
- **Primary Accent (`#F9B8C4`):** The luminous rose pink. Used for high-impact calls to action, active toggle borders, ambient glowing frames, and dynamic lighting ripples.
- **Secondary (`#151011`):** The deep obsidian tone. Serves as the canvas for the hero section, the grounding footer, and high-emphasis project hero cards.
- **Tertiary (`#B86B77`):** High-contrast deep rose. Reserved strictly for small-format category eyebrows, badges, and focal typography that require contrast on blush cards.
- **Neutral Surface (`#F6ECEB`):** The foundation of the primary content sections, paired with `#EFE3E2` for elevated bento modules and cards.
- **Ink & Readability:** Never use pure `#000000` text. High-contrast typography on light surfaces relies on `#231819` (deep warm ink), with secondary annotations utilizing `rgba(35, 24, 25, 0.65)`. On dark obsidian layers, text switches to `#FBF4F4` with supporting metadata in `rgba(251, 244, 244, 0.68)`.

## Typography

The typography pairings contrast the poetic editorial sensibility of `Playfair Display` with the structural precision of `Plus Jakarta Sans`.

- **Editorial Headings:** Display scales utilize tight negative tracking (`-0.02em`) and compact line heights to create a sculptural magazine layout. Headings are intended to be set in mixed casing; reserve italicization for accent quotes, emotional descriptors, or testimonial bodies.
- **Eyebrow Meta:** Section identifiers and role tags use uppercase tracking (`0.18em`) at reduced scales (11px) with bold weights (600), delivering crisp informational guidance without competing with nearby display serif headlines.
- **Body Rhythm:** General body and bio copy is restricted to readable column widths (maximum `48ch` to `60ch`) with open leading (`1.6` to `1.65`) to ensure effortless legibility against soft blush surfaces.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon a 12-column responsive fluid grid constrained to a centralized maximum width of `1240px` with dynamic outer canvas gutters scaling at `5vw`.

### Spatial Structure
- **Section Pacing:** Primary editorial content sections maintain generous vertical breathing room (`padding: 6rem 5vw`). The hero stage commands a minimum height of `92svh` to form an immediate cinematic impact.
- **Bento Grid Ratios:** Internal content is structured into asymmetrical two-column modules (`1.05fr | 0.95fr` or `1.15fr | 0.85fr`), using consistent gaps of `1.75rem` (`28px`).
- **Responsive Stacking:**
  - **Desktop (> 900px):** Multi-column bento layouts, horizontal narrative flows, and floating split views.
  - **Tablet (641px - 899px):** Bento modules collapse to single-column flows; internal grids retain horizontal alignment where possible.
  - **Mobile (≤ 640px):** All split columns collapse strictly to 1 column. Outer margins compress to `1rem` and section padding scales to `space-2xl` (`4rem`).

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids heavy, muddy drop-shadows, prioritizing luminous, translucent depth layers and soft atmospheric halos that make UI elements appear weightless.

### Depth Techniques
- **Luminous Rose Ambient Glows:** Buttons and highlighted cards emit an ethereal, tinted aura (`box-shadow: 0 0 24px rgba(249, 184, 196, 0.42)`), which expands softly to `34px` on hover.
- **Tonal Bento Stacking:** Surfaces step up in brightness and warmth rather than vertical lift. On the blush base (`#F6ECEB`), elevated cards leverage `#EFE3E2` bounded by razor-thin `1px` translucent outlines (`rgba(35, 24, 25, 0.09)`).
- **Rim-Lit Viewport Boundaries:** The primary hero viewport and floating bento blocks are accented with hairline borders carrying a soft rose shimmer (`1px solid rgba(249, 184, 196, 0.35)`).
- **Translucent Glass Pillars:** Secondary controls and navigation nodes utilize glassmorphic overlays (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`) accompanied by subtle backdrop blurs (`8px` to `12px`).

## Shapes

The shape system employs deliberate contrasts: generous, architectural card corners are combined with soft, pill-shaped interactive triggers.

### Geometry Hierarchy
- **Pill Geometry (`9999px`):** Reserved for interactive entities—buttons, affiliation badges, category chips, navigation links, and dynamic slide switchers.
- **Architectural Cards (`28px` / `var(--radius-xl)`):** Standard corner radius for all bento blocks, project showcases, testimonial containers, and collaboration cards.
- **Feature Frames (`20px` to `22px`):** Utilized for photographic media insets, avatar portrait masks, and interior showcase strips.
- **Hero & Footer Enclosures (`36px`):** Applied exclusively to the outer interface transition points—the bottom curve of the hero canvas and the top curve of the grounding obsidian footer.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary Button:** Luminous rose background (`#F9B8C4`), text in deep ink (`#231819`, 12px uppercase, weight 600, tracking `0.08em`), with full pill radius (`999px`). Features a default aura of `0 0 24px rgba(249, 184, 196, 0.42)`. On hover, elements translate `-2px` vertically with an amplified glow (`0 0 34px`).
- **Ghost & Secondary Button:** Fully transparent or glass surface (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`), 1px hairline border (`rgba(35, 24, 25, 0.15)` on light, `rgba(249, 184, 196, 0.35)` on dark), with subtle hover fills.
- **Arrow Affordance:** Text links and CTAs feature trailing glyphs (`→` or `↗`) that translate `4px` horizontally on hover using `cubic-bezier(.22, 1, .36, 1)`.

### Bento Cards & Containers
- **Light Bento Container:** `#EFE3E2` surface fill, `28px` corner radius, `40px` internal padding, bounded by a `1px` border of `rgba(35, 24, 25, 0.09)`.
- **Dark Feature Container:** Obsidian gradient (`linear-gradient(145deg, #231819, #151011)`), `28px` corner radius, with a subtle top-right radial rose glow and `1px` border of `rgba(249, 184, 196, 0.2)`.
- **Hover Motion:** On hover, cards smoothly lift `-6px` with an expanded shadow (`0 24px 48px rgba(35, 24, 25, 0.09)`).

### Chips & Badges
- **Affiliation & Status Badges:** Pill-shaped (`999px`), `10px 22px` padding, set with `1px solid rgba(35, 24, 25, 0.09)`, subtle porcelain backgrounds, and `12px` semi-bold type.
- **Role Switcher Pills:** Segmented pill container with an active luminous background (`rgba(249, 184, 196, 0.14)`), enclosed in a `1px solid #F9B8C4` active stroke.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs:** Subtle tinted background (`#EFE3E2` or obsidian translucent), rounded pill or `14px` border radius, padding `14px 20px`. Border starts at `1px solid rgba(35, 24, 25, 0.12)` and transitions smoothly to `#F9B8C4` with a soft `0 0 12px rgba(249, 184, 196, 0.35)` focus halo.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Rounded (`6px` for checkbox, circular for radio), `18px` diameter, hairline stroke. Active state transitions to `#F9B8C4` fill with deep `#231819` checkmark indicators.

### Lists & Micro-Details
- **Editorial Star Bullets:** Feature lists use a signature four-point star ornament (`✦`) in vibrant rose (`#C76D7E`), vertically centered against the list copy.
- **Dividers:** Hairline dividers between list items use `1px solid rgba(35, 24, 25, 0.09)` on light cards and `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` on obsidian surfaces.
